# Polls
In this activity, participants silently cast their votes on a variety of questions, hopefully spurring discussion!

1. Put up the [Poll Sheets](./Assets/PollQuestions.docx) around the room
1. Give each participant has a sheet of [dot stickers](https://www.avery.com/products/labels/5472) when they arrive
1. Encourage participants to stick their stickers in the quadrants that best reflect their feelings
1. Encourage them to discuss the topics with one another
1. After the voting period ends, go through the results as a group!
